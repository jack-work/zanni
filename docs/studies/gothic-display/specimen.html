Jacquard 12

The same drawing as Jacquard 24 on a grid half as fine: 21 cells per em instead of 43. At 21px one cell is exactly one CSS pixel, so every edge is solid. The x-height is the 8 rows Jacquard 24 shows at 19 to 20px, but the e counter stays open and n/u sit twice as far apart (0.20 against 0.10). Same designer, glyph set and licence text, so it is the smallest step that still reads as the current face. Cost: 21px and 42px are its only crisp sizes, so the 26px mark either drops to 21px or stays soft.

Jacquarda Bastarda 9

A bastarda cut on a 13 cell em, lighter and more open than the Jacquards. Ink density matches EB Garamond body (0.30 against 0.42), n/u are 0.44 apart and i/l 0.80. The x-height is 6 rows at the native 13px, so a 13px panel title reads small; at 26px (x-height 12) it is crisp and handsome but wide-set and light, nearer a pixel script than a textura. Suits the mark better than 16 to 20px titles.

Not Jam Blackletter 13

A 13 cell textura with a tall 8 row x-height, 2 cell stems and the most distinct lowercase of the set (c/e 0.40, n/u 0.55, a/o 0.70). Crisp at 13px, where its x-height equals Jacquard 24 at 19px. Heavier and chunkier than Jacquard, and the ascenders are short (1.25 times the x-height), so word shapes lean on the wide spacing. Coverage is ASCII plus the sharp s; accents would fall back to Garamond. CC0, 5 KB. Its advances are rounded in a 1024 unit em, so a long run at 13px drifts 0.008px per glyph and picks up a few soft edges (27% against 0% for the others).

Not Jam Blackletter 16

Same author and style at 16 cells per em: 1024 divides by 16, so 16px is exact and 32px is 2x. Covers Latin-1 and Latin Extended-A (308 codepoints). The x-height is only 7 rows and the ascenders equal the cap height (9), so it reads flatter than NJ13 and Jacquard, and it has the heaviest stems of the set (0.29 of the x-height). The coverage-safe sibling of NJ13.
