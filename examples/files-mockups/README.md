# files.kelliher.info, UI mockups (step 1)

Three static directions for the upload page (`/_upload/`) and the file explorer
(the lister), phone first. Invented data only. Each page is one self-contained
HTML file: zanni's gesso, rotolo and fontpack inlined, placeholder pictures
generated by `lib.py`, nothing fetched.

| direction | idea | upload | files |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| copione | the list, kept and tidied | `copione.html#u-sending` | `copione.html#f-folder` |
| provino | the contact sheet: tiles that develop | `provino.html#u-sending` | `provino.html#f-folder` |
| sipario | one number, one button, a curtain | `sipario.html#u-sending` | `sipario.html#f-folder` |

`moodboard.html` holds the references: 17 sources, each with its link, date
evidence, what was taken and what was skipped. Every screen carries a `from:`
note that links to its card. The third-party captures are not committed (keel
mirrors every branch to public GitHub): `python3 build.py` writes
`moodboard-local.html` with them inlined, from `refs/`, both untracked.

## States

Open a page and use the bar at the top, or put the state in the hash.

- upload: `u-ready u-sending u-paused u-away u-reload u-problem u-done`
- files: `f-root f-folder f-photo f-heic f-video f-pdf f-empty`

`u-away` is the page coming back after Safari backgrounded it. `u-reload` is
the worse case: the phone discarded the page, the file handles are gone, and
she has to choose the same files again. Add `?shot` to hide the bar and the
notes, `?scheme=dark` or `?scheme=light` to force a scheme.

## What the mockups assume

- Parts of 16 MB, three in flight, resume per part, Cloudflare's 100 MB
  request cap. No part is ever near it.
- The explorer pages contain no script. The lister's policy is
  `script-src 'none'`, so previews open with `:target` here and would be
  separate server-rendered pages in practice. Thumbnails need `img-src 'self'`
  added to that policy; today it falls back to `default-src 'none'`.
- The upload page keeps its policy (`script-src 'self'`, no inline style):
  progress is set through custom properties from script.
- The per-user view hides `users/<name>/`. She sees "Your files" as the root.
- HEIC is stored as sent, content type `image/heic`. The directions differ in
  how it is previewed:
  - copione: small copy made on the phone at upload time, used for thumbnails
    and as the non-Apple preview.
  - provino: the phone makes a thumbnail and a 1600 px JPEG; every device can
    show every photo; Download gives the original.
  - sipario: the server makes the JPEG after upload; new HEIC shows "preview on
    its way" for about a minute.
- Safari on iPhone: a wake lock keeps the screen on while sending (needs
  HTTPS; Immich ships it for web since v3.1.0). Not tested on a device.

## Build

    python3 build.py                  # the html files, plus moodboard-local.html
    node tools/shoot.js               # shots/<direction>/<screen>-<390|1280>.png
    TALL=1 node tools/shoot.js        # also full-length phone shots

`tools/shoot.js` needs playwright-core and a Chromium (see `tools/capture.js`
for how the references were captured). Shots are Chromium, not Safari.
